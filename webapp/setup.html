Projector calibration

Drag each corner handle until the grid below looks like a perfect rectangle on the actual floor (open this page on the kiosk PC and look at the real projection while you drag, or have someone watch the floor and call out adjustments). Skip this step if you don't have the projector connected yet — it'll render un-warped until you calibrate later from this same page.
